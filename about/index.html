A second life for something beautiful

The doors already had a story.

One of our favorite parts of Petals & Panes is the story behind these doors.

When we were searching for vintage French doors for the greenhouse, our friend Azalea sent us a picture of some old doors she had in storage and told us we were welcome to have them.

We already loved them the moment we saw the picture. But when we went to pick them up, we found out they were originally from Bessie’s Inn here in Huntsville.

That made them even more special.
