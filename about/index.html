The doors that started it all

A piece of Huntsville history found a new home.

One of our favorite parts of Petals & Panes is the story behind these doors.

When we were searching for vintage French doors for the greenhouse, our friend Azalea sent us a picture of some old doors she had in storage and told us we were welcome to have them.

We already loved them the moment we saw the picture. But when we went to pick them up, we found out they were originally from Bessie’s Inn here in Huntsville.

These beautiful old doors had been removed during renovations, but instead of being forgotten, they found a new home here at Petals & Panes. Knowing they have been part of Huntsville history since the early 1900s makes them even more meaningful to us.

These doors were never meant to be forgotten. They were waiting for a place where their story could continue.
