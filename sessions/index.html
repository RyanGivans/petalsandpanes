Petals & Panes is more than one backdrop. It is a collection of thoughtfully styled spaces that can shift with the season, the light, and the story you want to tell.

Use the glasshouse for timeless natural-light portraits, move into the garden for softer editorial images, or build an entire gallery around the vintage details tucked throughout the property.

The glasshouse

The heart of the property.

Vintage panes, soft neutral tones, and natural light create a setting that feels romantic without overpowering the people in front of the camera.

It works beautifully for motherhood, maternity, family, branding, seniors, and seasonal sessions.

The garden arch

A frame inside the frame.

Covered in vines and seasonal blooms, the garden arch gives portraits an immediate sense of depth and place. It is one of the easiest spots on the property to turn a simple pose into a finished image.

Photographs that feel like a place

Every corner gives you something different.

A session can move from glass and greenery to vintage props, flowers, and shaded garden paths without ever feeling like the same backdrop repeated over and over.
